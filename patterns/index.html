🗂️ The families at a glance

Patterns come in families. Each family answers one kind of question.

FamilyThe question it answersPatterns in this lab
🏭 CreationalHow do I make objects without hard-wiring which one?Factory, Builder, Singleton (+ Abstract Factory, Prototype)
🔧 StructuralHow do I fit pieces together?Adapter, Decorator, Facade, Proxy, Composite (+ Bridge, Flyweight)
💬 BehavioralHow do objects work and talk together?Strategy, Observer, Command, State, Template Method, Chain of Responsibility (+ Mediator, Memento, Iterator, Visitor)
☁️ ArchitectureHow do I design a whole system that stays reliable?Repository, CQRS, Event Sourcing, Saga, Circuit Breaker & Retry, Cache-Aside, Strangler Fig (+ Outbox, Bulkhead)
